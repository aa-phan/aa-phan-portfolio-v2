## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor, **I want to** open the deployed project URL and see a fast, themed hero in light or dark, **so that** hosting, CI, theming and motion risks are retired before content is built.

Implements D-09 (TextEffect on the role line, both libraries honoring useMotionPreference) and completes D-08 (thumb snaps with reduced motion).

Purpose: MOT-01, the reduced-motion half of success criterion 5.
