## Phase Goal

**As a** recruiter on a phone, **I want to** find Aaron's role, experience, projects, resume and contact details within seconds, **so that** I can decide whether to reach out without hunting for information.

<objective>
Collect every owner-only input in one stop at the start of the phase, so the content modules are typed once from a final inventory. The tracer drafts the three featured write-ups from resume facts and proves mechanically that they invent no figures. The checkpoint asks the owner for the four inputs CONTEXT reserves for him: project repo URLs (D-13), write-up approval (D-14), the About line about the night side (D-15) and the phone-free resume export (D-16). The last task records the answers in the inventory and checks the PDF.

Purpose: CNT-02 (complete content from the reviewed inventory) and CNT-04 (the served resume is the owner's phone-free copy).
Output: an updated content inventory and the owner's PDF at public/resume.pdf.

Ordering: wave 1, in parallel with plan 03-01. Its files never overlap 03-01's. It runs no build and no repo-wide format write: it formats only the inventory with npx prettier --write followed by that one path.
</objective>
