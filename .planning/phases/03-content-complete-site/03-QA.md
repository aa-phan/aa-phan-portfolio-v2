## Fix list

The critic's ranked list (by score gained), as returned.

1. **Project cards are empty (d-03, m-03, d-projects-dark): Design and Content.** Each card is about 400 px of flat grey with the title stuck in the bottom-left corner. Put something in it: a screenshot, a diagram, or a one-line outcome in display type (for example "TikTok recipe → Kroger cart"). Cap the title at two lines; "MediaDash: Content Recommendation Hub" runs to four lines in d-03.
2. **The concept is hidden behind the toggle (d-01 compared with d-hero-dark): Creativity.** In light mode nothing says PHVNTM until the footer caption. Hint at the alter ego on the default screen, for example by labelling the theme toggle "PHAN / PHVNTM" or adding a hero line beside the toggle, so a first-time visitor finds the switch.
3. **Missing social meta (audit): Markup.** Add og:title, og:description, an og:image that loads (1200×630, the wordmark poster), twitter:card=summary_large_image and a canonical link. That gets Markup to 8. Adding robots.txt and sitemap.xml lifts Semantics/SEO by about 1.
4. **Nav hover is invisible (h-11): Animations and Usability.** Use the active-state indigo underline from d-02 as the hover too, drawing in from the left. Also give the cards a hover that shows on the dark section (h-02 to h-05): lighten the card fill one step or slide the title up, rather than a shadow nobody can see.
5. **The 404 cuts straight to home (d-90-a/b/c are identical): Animations.** Add a continuous transition, for example the collapsed A in the 404 wordmark springing back up as the page hands over to home.
6. **The mobile bottom pill covers content (m-01, m-04): Usability and Responsive.** Add bottom padding equal to the pill height plus 16 px at the end of each section, and put a background-colored gradient scrim behind the pill so text doesn't run under it.
7. **The email block is indented (d-07, m-06): Design.** The label and address sit 16 px right of "The fastest way to reach me is email." Drop the button's inner padding at rest so the text lines up with the column. Then pad the hover flood so the "Copy email" label stops touching the pill edge (h-06 hover).
8. **The footer wordmark doesn't span the grid (d-07): Design.** PHAN stops about 95 px short of the right margin. Size it to the full 29 to 1411 px width (fit-text), as the dark-mode PHVNTM nearly does in d-footer-dark.
9. **Skills is a text wall (d-05, m-04): Design and Content.** It's set at display size, so on mobile it runs past 12 lines. Make the category labels small overlines and set the items as a smaller list in columns, or cut it to the stack he actually uses (Go comes first in the hero line).
10. **The hero's right column is empty (d-01): Design.** About 700×450 px above the CTAs holds nothing. Either let the h1 run wider (bigger display size) or move the "Graduating" and "Last role" facts up into that space.
11. **Focus rings sit tight on the nav text (k-03, k-04): Accessibility polish.** Add a 4 px outline-offset and round the outline to match the pill buttons.
